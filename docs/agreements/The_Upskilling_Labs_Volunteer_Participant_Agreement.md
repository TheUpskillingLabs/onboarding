**THE UPSKILLING LABS**

Volunteer Participation Agreement

*A policy acknowledgment for all volunteers and participants*

The Upskilling Labs, Inc. ("The Labs") is a community-driven
organization supporting people navigating career transitions in a
changing labor market. We do this through peer-driven learning cycles
and open, public-facing projects. Volunteers are essential to making it
work.

By signing this Volunteer Participation Agreement ("Agreement"), you
confirm that you have read, understood, and agree to the terms and
expectations set out below. This document is a policy acknowledgment,
not a contract of employment or a bilateral contract. It establishes the
terms under which The Labs welcomes your participation.

Volunteers contribute across a range of functions, including
facilitation, strategy, technology, content, design, and community
support.

1. What You Gain as a Volunteer
===============================

Volunteering with The Labs is an exchange. While you contribute your
time and skills without financial compensation, The Labs is committed to
making your participation genuinely valuable. By volunteering, you can
expect:

-   **Learning and Skill Development.** Access to real-world practice in
    > facilitation, content development, technology, community building,
    > and other applied skills. You will work on actual projects with
    > tangible outcomes, not simulated exercises.

-   **Networking and Community.** Connection to a growing network of
    > professionals, practitioners, and community members. Labs events,
    > cohorts, and projects are designed to create meaningful peer
    > relationships.

-   **Volunteer Experience and Recognition.** Verified volunteer
    > experience you can reference on your resume and professional
    > profiles, including LinkedIn. With permission, The Labs may
    > publicly acknowledge your contributions across its platforms.

-   **Portfolio and Published Work.** Many volunteer contributions
    > result in open-source resources that are publicly available and
    > attributable. Content contributors, R&D volunteers, and workshop
    > facilitators may be credited on published materials, giving you
    > shareable, public-facing proof of your work.

-   **Access to Labs Programs and Resources.** Participation in Labs
    > programming, workshops, and learning cycles that are otherwise
    > available to enrolled participants. Volunteers are embedded in the
    > community, not separate from it.

-   **Mission-Driven Impact.** The direct experience of contributing to
    > something that matters. The Labs works at the intersection of
    > workforce development, community resilience, and open knowledge
    > --- your work here has real consequences for real people
    > navigating career transitions.

The sections below set out the mutual expectations that make this
exchange work well for everyone.

2. Your Status as a Volunteer
=============================

By participating, you acknowledge that:

a)  You are volunteering your time freely and are not an employee,
    > contractor, partner, agent, or representative of The Labs.

b)  You will not receive compensation or benefits for your volunteer
    > activities.

c)  Any expense reimbursement must be pre-approved in writing by The
    > Labs before it is incurred.

d)  You have received and reviewed The Labs' onboarding materials and
    > understand the expectations applicable to your role.

3. Participation and Conduct
============================

While participating as a volunteer, you agree to:

a)  Engage in a manner consistent with The Labs' community standards,
    > onboarding materials, and any applicable code of conduct.

b)  Treat all participants, staff, and fellow volunteers with respect,
    > and avoid harassment, discrimination, or retaliation of any kind.

c)  Not use The Labs' community, events, or platforms to directly sell
    > your services or paid offerings without written consent from The
    > Labs. Reasonable professional networking is welcome; high-pressure
    > sales behavior is not.

d)  Exercise reasonable care when providing guidance, feedback,
    > facilitation, or technical support, and avoid representing
    > yourself as speaking on behalf of The Labs unless explicitly
    > authorized to do so.

e)  Follow any role-specific guidelines, editorial standards,
    > facilitation protocols, or technical contribution practices
    > communicated to you during onboarding or in writing by The Labs.

f)  Disclose any conflict of interest promptly (see Section 9).

4. Confidentiality
==================

3.1 What is Confidential
------------------------

In the course of your volunteer work, you may have access to non-public
information about The Labs, its participants, partners, or operations.
This includes personal data about participants, internal financial
information, grant applications in progress, private cohort discussions,
and technical infrastructure details ("Confidential Information").

3.2 Your Obligations
--------------------

You agree to:

a)  use Confidential Information only for your volunteer activities
    > within The Labs,

b)  not share it with anyone outside The Labs without written
    > authorization, and

c)  take reasonable steps to keep it secure.

3.3 What Is Not Confidential
----------------------------

Information that is publicly available, that you already knew
independently, or that you receive from a third party without
restriction is not Confidential Information. Much of what The Labs
produces is intentionally public --- open repositories, published blog
posts, and event recaps are not confidential.

3.4 Duration
------------

Your confidentiality obligations continue for two (2) years after your
volunteer participation ends. Your obligations with respect to personal
data about participants continue for as long as you retain that data.

5. Photos, Video, and Your Personal Data
========================================

4.1 Photo and Video
-------------------

Your likeness may be captured in photos or videos at Labs events and
used to promote the community and its programs. If you prefer not to be
photographed or filmed, please let a staff member know before or at the
event. You may also opt out at any time by notifying The Labs in
writing. Any opt-out applies prospectively from the date we receive your
request and does not require removal of materials already published or
distributed.

4.2 Your Information
--------------------

The Labs collects and retains certain information about volunteers (such
as name, email address, photo, and professional profile) to administer
the volunteer program and promote the community. We will not sell your
personal information to third parties. You may request access to,
correction of, or deletion of your information by contacting us in
writing. Upon the end of your participation, we will retain only what is
necessary for legal, tax, or recordkeeping purposes.

6. Intellectual Property and Contributions
==========================================

5.1 Your Existing Work
----------------------

You retain ownership of any work, frameworks, templates, or materials
you created before joining The Labs or developed entirely outside your
volunteer activities ("Your Background IP"). Nothing here transfers
ownership of Your Background IP to The Labs.

5.2 What You Contribute to Open Projects
----------------------------------------

The Labs produces open-source resources that are free for anyone to use,
share, and adapt. If you contribute to these resources --- whether
written content, templates, workshop materials, slide decks, code,
designs, or other materials --- you acknowledge that:

-   Those contributions may be used, shared, modified, and built upon by
    > others under the applicable open-source license for that project.

-   You are representing that you have the right to contribute those
    > materials and that doing so does not violate any obligations you
    > have to a current or former employer or client.

-   You will not later attempt to restrict use of materials you have
    > already contributed to an open project.

5.3 Sharing Without Contributing
--------------------------------

If you share Your Background IP as a teaching example or reference
without intending it to become a contribution, label it clearly as "Not
a Contribution / No License Granted." Avoid posting such materials in
open project repositories or channels where reuse is expected.

5.4 The Labs' Materials
-----------------------

The Labs' name, logos, trademarks, curricula, and internal documents
remain the property of The Labs. You may use these only as needed to
carry out your volunteer role and may not publish, sell, or distribute
them outside The Labs without written permission.

7. Personal Safety and Risk
===========================

Some volunteer activities --- including in-person workshops, events, and
meetups --- involve inherent risks, including the possibility of
personal injury, property damage, or illness. By participating, you
acknowledge these risks and agree to exercise reasonable care for your
own safety and the safety of others.

The Labs does not provide workers' compensation, health insurance,
disability coverage, or general liability coverage for volunteers. We
encourage you to confirm that your own personal insurance covers your
volunteer activities before participating in any in-person event.

8. Conflicts of Interest
========================

Please disclose any material conflicts of interest promptly, including
competitive relationships, financial interest in a participant's
project, or intent to recruit participants into your own paid services
or employment. Do not use The Labs primarily as a solicitation or
recruiting channel. Reasonable professional networking is welcome;
high-pressure sales or recruitment behavior is not.

9. Publicity
============

You may be listed on The Labs' website and in our communications (name,
role, title, organization, and photo) unless you opt out in writing. You
may describe yourself as a volunteer at The Upskilling Labs on
professional profiles such as LinkedIn. Please do not use The Labs' name
or marks in a way that implies our organizational endorsement of your
work or services without written permission.

10. No Guarantees
=================

Volunteer activities are educational and community-based in nature.
Nothing in this Agreement constitutes legal, financial, medical, or
other regulated professional services, unless explicitly agreed in
writing and you are appropriately licensed to provide such services.

The Labs makes no guarantees regarding participant outcomes, employment,
funding, project success, or the continued operation of any program or
volunteer role.

11. Limitation of Liability
===========================

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, The Labs will not be
liable for indirect, incidental, special, or consequential damages
arising from your volunteer participation or any Labs activities. The
Labs' total liability to you under this Agreement will not exceed \$100.

Similarly, your liability to The Labs arising from your volunteer
activities --- other than for intentional misconduct or fraud --- will
not exceed \$100. Nothing here limits either party's responsibility for
gross negligence, willful misconduct, or punitive damages.

12. Updates to This Agreement
=============================

The Labs may update these terms from time to time to reflect changes in
our programs, legal requirements, or community standards. When we do, we
will notify you in writing. If you continue participating after
receiving notice of an update, that constitutes your acceptance of the
revised terms. If you do not accept a revision, you may end your
participation by notifying us in writing.

13. General
===========

a)  This Agreement is governed by the laws of the District of Columbia,
    > without regard to conflict-of-law rules.

b)  If any provision of this Agreement is found to be unenforceable, the
    > remaining provisions stay in effect.

c)  This Agreement represents the full understanding between you and The
    > Labs regarding your volunteer participation and supersedes any
    > prior informal understandings on this subject.
