**The Upskilling Labs --- Volunteer Mentor Agreement**
------------------------------------------------------

This Volunteer Mentor Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and
between **The Upskilling Labs, Inc.** ("**The Labs**") and the
undersigned volunteer mentor ("**Mentor**"), effective as of the date of
signature ("**Effective Date**").

### **1) Purpose**

The Labs runs peer-driven learning cycles and open, public-facing
projects. Mentor will volunteer time to support participants
("Participants") through guidance, feedback, introductions, and
technical or strategic mentorship.

### **2) Volunteer Status (No Compensation)**

-   Mentor serves **voluntarily** and is **not** an employee,
    > contractor, partner, agent, or joint venturer of The Labs.

-   Mentor will **not** receive compensation or benefits.

-   Any expense reimbursement must be **pre-approved in writing** by The
    > Labs.

### **3) Term and Termination**

-   This Agreement continues until terminated.

-   Either party may terminate this Agreement **at any time**, with or
    > without cause, by written notice.

-   Sections intended to survive (including IP, confidentiality,
    > disclaimers, and liability) will survive termination.

### **4) Mentor Role and Conduct**

Mentor agrees to:

-   Provide guidance in line with our [[Mentor
    > Guidelines]{.underline}](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Mbj81L_7MqTvUE0in63Q3lDKg7Yxqac0/edit#heading=h.aqkv7hmugt4l),
    > in a manner consistent with The Labs' community standards and any
    > applicable code of conduct.

-   Avoid harassment, discrimination, or retaliation.

-   Not directly sell their services or other paid offerings through the
    > Upskilling Labs community and events without written consent from
    > The Labs. Reasonable professional networking is allowed;
    > high-pressure sales behavior is not.

-   Use reasonable care when providing advice and avoid representing
    > Mentor as speaking on behalf of The Labs unless explicitly
    > authorized

### **5) Confidentiality and Privacy**

**5.1 Confidential Information.** "Confidential Information" means
non-public information The Labs or Participants share with Mentor that
is marked or reasonably understood to be confidential (e.g., personal
data, internal operations, private cohort discussions, non-public
partner info).

**5.2 Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include
information that:

-   is publicly available without breach,

-   was already known to Mentor without obligation,

-   is independently developed without use of Confidential Information,
    > or

-   is lawfully obtained from a third party.

**5.3 Obligations.** Mentor will:

-   use Confidential Information only for mentoring within The Labs,

-   not disclose it to third parties except as authorized in writing,
    > and

-   take reasonable steps to protect it.

**5.4 Open Work is Not Confidential.** Mentor understands that many Labs
activities are conducted in public (e.g., open repos, public demos).
Information intentionally posted to open channels is **not**
Confidential Information.

**5.5 Photo and video release.** You agree that your likeness may be
captured via photo and video at events associated with The Upskilling
Labs, and be used by The Upskilling Labs to help promote the community
and event.

### **6) Intellectual Property (IP) --- Mentor Pre-Existing IP Protected**

**6.1 Mentor Retains Ownership.** Mentor retains all right, title, and
interest in:

-   any inventions, templates, frameworks, code, materials, trademarks,
    > know-how, or content created **before** this Agreement or
    > developed **outside** Labs activities ("Mentor Background IP").

**6.2 No Transfer by Default.** Except for the open-source license grant
in Section 7, nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of Mentor
Background IP to The Labs or Participants.

### **7) Open Source Contributions --- License Grant for What Mentor Introduces**

The Labs Participants may work on projects intended to be **open
source** ("Open Projects").

**7.1 Project Licenses Control.** Each Open Project may be governed by
an applicable open-source license (e.g., MIT, Apache-2.0, GPL) and/or
contribution terms posted in the project repository ("Project License").

**7.2 Mentor Contribution = Permission to Use.** If Mentor
**contributes** to an Open Project---whether by code, documentation,
designs, datasets, prompts, playbooks, or other materials
("Contributions")---Mentor agrees that:

-   Mentor is **granting a license** (or sublicensable rights as
    > required) for those Contributions to be used, modified,
    > distributed, and otherwise exploited under the **Project
    > License**, and

-   Mentor will not later attempt to revoke those rights for
    > Contributions already made available to the Open Project.

**7.3 You Only Contribute What You Can License.** Mentor represents that
for any Contribution:

-   Mentor either owns it or has sufficient rights to grant the license
    > described above, and

-   Mentor will not knowingly contribute materials that violate
    > third-party rights (including employer/contractor IP obligations).

**7.4 Optional: Marking Background IP.** If Mentor shares Mentor
Background IP as an example or teaching aid **without** intending it to
become a Contribution, Mentor should clearly label it "**Not a
Contribution / No License Granted**." The Labs will make reasonable
efforts to respect such labeling; however, Mentor should avoid posting
such materials into Open Project repositories or channels where reuse is
expected.

### **8) The Labs IP and Materials**

-   The Labs retains ownership of its name, logos, trademarks,
    > curricula, and internal documents ("Labs Materials").

-   Mentor receives a limited, revocable, non-exclusive license to use
    > Labs Materials **only** as needed to perform mentoring. Mentor may
    > not publish, sell, or distribute Labs Materials outside The Labs
    > without written permission.

### **9) Publicity**

Mentor may be listed publicly as a mentor (name, title, organization,
and photo) unless Mentor opts out in writing. Mentor may list themselves
as a "Mentor" at "The Upskilling Labs" on LinkedIn. Mentor may not use
The Labs' name or marks in a way that implies endorsement without
written permission.

### **10) Conflicts of Interest**

Mentor will disclose any material conflicts (e.g., mentoring a direct
competitor relationship, or recruiting Participants into paid services)
and agrees not to use The Labs primarily as a solicitation channel.
Reasonable professional networking is allowed; high-pressure sales
behavior is not.

### **11) Disclaimers**

-   Mentorship is educational and community-based. Mentor is not
    > providing legal, medical, financial, or other regulated
    > professional services through The Labs unless explicitly agreed in
    > writing and appropriately licensed.

-   The Labs makes no guarantees regarding outcomes, employment,
    > funding, or project success.

### **12) Limitation of Liability**

To the maximum extent permitted by law:

-   The Labs will not be liable for indirect, incidental, special,
    > consequential, or punitive damages arising out of the mentoring
    > relationship.

-   The Labs' total liability under this Agreement will not exceed
    > **\$100**.

### **13) Indemnity (Basic)**

Mentor agrees to indemnify and hold harmless The Labs from third-party
claims arising from Mentor's intentional misconduct or knowing
infringement of third-party IP rights through Mentor Contributions.

### **14) Governing Law**

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the **District of Columbia**,
without regard to conflict-of-law rules.

### **15) Miscellaneous**

-   This Agreement is the entire agreement on this subject and
    > supersedes prior discussions.

-   If any provision is unenforceable, the rest remains in effect.

-   This Agreement may be signed electronically and in counterparts.
